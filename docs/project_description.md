Hotel Reservation System — Project Description

1. Project Overview

The Hotel Reservation System is a web application designed to help a single hotel manage room availability, guest reservations, check-in and check-out, billing, and hotel operations.

The system will allow guests to manage their bookings online while providing hotel staff with tools to handle daily operations.

2. Main Users

Guest: Searches for available room types and creates, views, modifies, or cancels their own reservations.

Receptionist: Manages guest records, handles reservations when needed, assigns rooms during check-in, processes check-out, and manages billing.

Hotel Manager: Manages rooms, room types, rates, and hotel operations.

Housekeeping Staff: Updates the cleaning and operational status of rooms.

3. Main Functionalities

Manage room types, physical rooms, and room rates.

Search room availability based on dates and room type.

Create, view, modify, and cancel reservations.

Prevent overbooking.

Store and manage guest records.

Assign physical rooms during check-in.

Manage guest check-in and check-out.

Generate bills and record simulated payments.

Track room cleaning and operational status.

Display basic hotel operational information.

4. Scope and Assumptions

The system will manage a single hotel.

Guests will reserve a room type rather than a specific physical room.

Physical rooms will be assigned during check-in.

Guests will be able to manage their own reservations.

Receptionists will also be able to manage reservations.

Cancellation, refund, and billing policies will initially be simple.

Payments will be simulated without processing real financial transactions.

The system will use demonstration data.

5. Out of Scope

Real payment processing.

Real SMS and email integrations.

Integration with external hotel booking platforms.

Management of multiple hotels.

6. Details to Define Later

Guest authentication and account management.

Exact cancellation and refund policies.

Detailed billing and tax calculations.

Room assignment and availability rules.

Handling rooms that become unavailable unexpectedly.

These details will be refined during requirements engineering and system design.

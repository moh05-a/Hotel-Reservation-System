**Version:** v0.2.0-design  
**Increment:** Room Management  
**Requirements:** FR-RM-01–FR-RM-05

## 1. Design Task

Design the room management module for the Hotel Reservation System, including room types, physical rooms, nightly rates, validation rules, and database relationships.

## 2. AI Assistance

ChatGPT was used to assist with:

- Preparing the Room Management Design document.
- Defining room attributes, relationships, and validation rules.
- Creating the Room Management Class Diagram using PlantUML.
- Preparing an editable Room Management ERD using Draw.io.
- Reviewing consistency between the diagrams and requirements.

## 3. Design Decisions

- Each physical room belongs to exactly one Room Type.
- Room Types contain maximum guest capacity and nightly rate.
- All room numbers must be unique.
- Only Hotel Managers can modify room types, physical rooms, and rates.
- Nightly rates use JOD.
- Room deactivation must preserve reservation and stay integrity.
- Cleaning and operational statuses are represented separately, pending team confirmation.

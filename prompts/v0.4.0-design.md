# Increment 4 — AI-Assisted Design Record

**Version:** v0.4.0-design  
**Increment:** Guest Management and History  
**Requirements:** FR-GS-01–FR-GS-04  
**Owner:** Yousef Taha

## 1. Design Task

Design guest record management for the Hotel Reservation System without introducing guest login accounts.

The design must support creating and maintaining guest information, searching for existing guests, viewing linked reservation/stay history, and safely identifying returning guests. It must prevent automatic merging of different people who share the same name or contact information.

## 2. AI Assistance

ChatGPT was used to assist with:

- Preparing detailed Guest Management use case descriptions.
- Designing the Guest class and its relationship to reservations and stays.
- Creating an editable Guest ERD in Draw.io.
- Creating a Guest Management Use Case Diagram in PlantUML.
- Reviewing guest matching, linking, access permissions, and alternative flows.

## 3. Main Design Decisions

- Guests do not register for website accounts.
- A Guest record has a persistent internal identifier and may be linked to multiple reservations and stays.
- Receptionists use authorized staff access to search, view, and update guest records and history.
- Matching names, phone numbers, or emails may suggest possible returning guests, but must not automatically merge or link records.
- A receptionist must verify a proposed match before linking a booking to an existing Guest record.
- Guest history must not become accessible through a single booking reference and secret code.

## 5. Review and Validation

The design was checked against FR-GS-01–04 and the project's account-free booking model. The use cases cover guest searches, profile access and updates, history viewing, returning-guest linking, and match verification.

The diagrams and documentation were submitted in [PR #9](https://github.com/moh05-a/Hotel-Reservation-System/pull/9), which was merged into `main`.

The exact staff verification evidence for a returning guest remains a team-level design detail. This is a design checkpoint only; application implementation and automated tests belong to the working increment.

## 6. Representative Prompts (Reconstructed)

The prompts below are cleaned reconstructions of the main design requests. They summarize the AI-assisted work and are **not verbatim conversation transcripts**.

**Prompt 1 — Guest management scope**

> Design Increment 4 (Guest Management) for our single-hotel reservation system using FR-GS-01–04. Guests have no website accounts. Explain how guest records are created, searched, updated, and connected to reservations and stay history. Keep the design consistent with the existing project requirements.

**Prompt 2 — Class diagram and ERD**

> Create an editable Guest class diagram in PlantUML and a Guest ERD in Draw.io. Include the Guest attributes, a stable internal guest ID, and the relationships needed to connect one guest to multiple reservations and stays. Do not add unnecessary entities or implementation features.

**Prompt 3 — Use cases and access control**

> Prepare a Guest Management Use Case Diagram and matching use case descriptions. Focus on the Receptionist's authorized actions, including searching, viewing, updating, linking returning guests, and viewing guest history. Include main flows, alternative flows, and denied-access cases.

**Prompt 4 — Returning-guest verification**

> Review how the system identifies a returning guest. A matching name, email, or phone number must not automatically merge two records. Show a safe process where the Receptionist checks a suggested match and explicitly approves the link before the reservation is associated with an existing Guest.

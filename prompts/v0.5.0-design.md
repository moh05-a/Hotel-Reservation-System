## 1. Design Task

Design the booking creation process for the Hotel Reservation System. Guests reserve a room type without creating an account. Proceeding to payment must create a temporary five-minute Pending hold, and a successful simulated payment is required before confirming the reservation.

The design must prevent overbooking, handle unsuccessful payments and hold expiration, and issue secure booking credentials only after confirmation. Physical room assignment remains part of Increment 7.

## 3. Main Design Decisions

- Guests book a room type, not a specific physical room.
- Proceeding to payment creates one concurrency-safe `Pending` hold for five minutes.
- An active Pending hold reduces room-type inventory for every booked night.
- Failed simulated payments may be retried without extending the original hold deadline.
- A reservation becomes `Confirmed` only after a successful simulated payment and a committed database update while the hold is still valid.
- Expired holds release inventory and cannot be confirmed by a late payment result.
- A unique booking reference and unpredictable secret code are issued after confirmation; only the secret's secure hash is stored.
- The initial cancellation policy gives a full simulated refund at least 24 hours before check-in and no refund for later eligible cancellations.
- An unchecked-in reservation becomes `NoShow` at noon the following day, with no late-arrival exception in the initial policy.

## 6. Representative Prompts (Reconstructed)

The prompts below are cleaned reconstructions of the main design requests. They are **not verbatim conversation transcripts**.

**Prompt 1 — Booking flow and temporary hold**

> Design Increment 5 (Reservation Creation) using FR-RS-01–03. A guest must be able to book a room type without an account. When the guest proceeds to simulated payment, create a five-minute Pending hold that temporarily reduces inventory. Explain validation, availability checks, and how the system avoids duplicate holds.

**Prompt 2 — Sequence Diagram**

> Create a PlantUML Sequence Diagram for reservation creation. Show the Guest, booking interface, backend, availability service, simulated payment service, and database. Include successful payment, failed payment with retry, hold expiration, database commit failure, and the point at which the reference and secret code are issued.

**Prompt 3 — State Diagram**

> Create a PlantUML State Diagram for the reservation lifecycle. Include Pending, Confirmed, Expired, Cancelled, CheckedIn, CheckedOut, and NoShow. Do not model a failed payment attempt as a separate reservation state. Explain the conditions that allow each transition.

**Prompt 4 — Concurrency and security review**

> Review the booking design for overbooking and access-control risks. Two guests must not be able to hold the last available unit of the same room type. Payment retries must use the original five-minute deadline, and booking credentials must not be issued before successful confirmation. Suggest safe transaction and idempotency rules without adding unnecessary implementation details.

**Prompt 5 — Booking policies**

> Document the initial booking policies in a concise Markdown file. Use hotel-local check-in at 14:00 and check-out at noon, a 24-hour cancellation/refund cutoff, and a NoShow deadline of noon on the following day with no late-arrival exception. Keep the rules consistent with Booking Management and Billing, which will be designed in later increments.

# Increment 6 — AI-Assisted Design Record

**Version:** v0.6.0-design  
**Increment:** Booking Viewing, Modification, and Cancellation  
**Requirements:** FR-RS-04–FR-RS-06, NFR-01, NFR-03, NFR-04  
**Owner:** Yousef Taha

## 1. Design Task

Design secure booking management for guests who do not have website accounts. A guest must enter the booking reference and matching secret code to access one reservation. An authorized Receptionist can manage bookings using staff permissions.

The design must cover booking viewing, eligible modifications, cancellation rules, inventory checks, simulated payment differences, and refunds without changing the established Increment 5 booking model.

## 2. AI Assistance

ChatGPT was used to assist with:

- Preparing Booking Management use case descriptions and access rules.
- Creating a Booking Management Use Case Diagram in PlantUML.
- Creating and reviewing booking management Sequence Diagrams.
- Splitting a long combined Sequence Diagram into three clearer diagrams for viewing, modification, and cancellation.
- Reviewing the 24-hour cutoff, availability rechecks, price differences, refunds, and concurrent changes.
- Clarifying the difference between updating a booking's permitted contact details and editing an entire Guest profile.

## 3. Main Design Decisions

- A guest's booking reference and secret code authorize access to that booking only.
- An authorized Receptionist can view or manage a booking through staff RBAC without the guest's secret code.
- Viewing a booking does not modify its status or reveal other guest records.
- Date changes require at least 24 hours before the original scheduled check-in and an availability check for all requested nights.
- A higher-priced modification requires successful simulated payment of the difference; a lower-priced modification results in a simulated refund after the eligible change is committed.
- A failed or conflicting modification must preserve the original booking and inventory.
- Cancellation is permitted before scheduled check-in. At least 24 hours before check-in gives a full simulated refund; later eligible cancellation gives no refund.
- The initial version excludes ordinary cancellation after check-in and guest-initiated room-type changes.
- Three separate Sequence Diagrams were preferred over one combined diagram to improve readability and simplify review.

## 6. Representative Prompts (Reconstructed)

The prompts below are cleaned reconstructions of the main design requests. They summarize the design workflow and are **not verbatim conversation transcripts**.

**Prompt 1 — Booking management scope**

> Design Increment 6 using FR-RS-04–06. Guests have no accounts and can manage only one booking using its booking reference and secret code. Authorized Receptionists use staff permissions instead. Prepare the use cases and rules for viewing, modifying, and cancelling confirmed bookings, and keep them consistent with Increment 5.

**Prompt 2 — Use Case Diagram**

> Create a PlantUML Use Case Diagram for booking management. Include Guest and Receptionist actors, authorization, viewing, modification, cancellation, change eligibility, date availability checks, and applicable simulated payment/refund actions. Use include and extend relationships only where they make sense.

**Prompt 3 — Separate Sequence Diagrams**

> The combined Booking Management Sequence Diagram is too long. Split it into three PlantUML Sequence Diagrams: View Booking, Modify Booking, and Cancel Booking. Keep the existing functionality and error paths, but make each diagram easier to read. Show how Receptionist authorization differs from guest reference-and-code verification.

**Prompt 4 — Modification and inventory safety**

> Review the Modify Booking flow. Date changes must respect the 24-hour cutoff and recheck inventory without losing the original reservation. Handle higher and lower prices using simulated payments or refunds. Include what happens if the payment succeeds but the booking update fails, and prevent concurrent changes from silently overwriting each other.

**Prompt 5 — Cancellation and documentation review**

> Review the Cancel Booking flow against the agreed policy: full simulated refund at least 24 hours before check-in, no refund for later eligible cancellations, and no ordinary cancellation after scheduled check-in. Also confirm that updating permitted booking contact details does not allow the guest to change the full Guest profile. Keep the documentation concise and consistent with related increments.

1. Design Task

Design the front-desk check-in and check-out workflow for the Hotel Reservation System.

At check-in, a Receptionist must find a confirmed reservation, manually compare the guest's presented identification with the booking information (simulated, no ID scanning or image storage), record that verification, and assign a suitable physical room of the reserved room type. At check-out, the Receptionist must end the active stay, record the departure, and trigger the related billing and room-readiness updates.

3. Main Design Decisions
Only a Confirmed reservation can be checked in; other statuses are rejected.
Identity verification is a manual, simulated step: the Receptionist records that the presented ID matches the authorized guest. No ID images or document numbers are stored.
Check-in is blocked until the verification confirmation is recorded.
The physical room is assigned at check-in, not at reservation creation. It must match the reserved room type, be ready, not be Out-of-Order, and have no conflicting active stay.
A successful check-in creates an active Stay linked to the reservation and room, records the check-in time, and writes an audit record of the staff action.
Only an active stay can be checked out. Check-out records the departure time, completes the stay, asks Billing to finalize the folio, and updates the room's readiness status.
Early/late check-out is applied through an agreed policy hook; the exact timing and fees remain to be confirmed (FR-CI-04 is marked TBD in the requirements).
The front-desk flow is staff-only and uses the staff roles from Increment 1.

6. Representative Prompts (Reconstructed)

The prompts below are cleaned reconstructions of the main design requests. They summarize the design workflow and are not verbatim conversation transcripts.

Prompt 1 — Check-in / check-out scope

Design Increment 7 (Check-in / Check-out) for our single-hotel reservation system using FR-CI-01–04. Guests have no accounts, and reservations are made for a room type, not a physical room. Explain how a Receptionist finds a confirmed reservation, verifies the guest, assigns a physical room, and later checks the guest out.

Prompt 2 — Manual identity verification

The identity check must be simulated. The Receptionist manually compares the guest's presented ID with the booking information and records the result. Do not add ID scanning or store ID images. Check-in must be blocked until this confirmation is recorded, and a wrong guest or an already checked-in reservation must be rejected.

Prompt 3 — Sequence Diagram

Create a PlantUML Sequence Diagram for check-in and check-out. Show the Receptionist, Staff UI, FastAPI backend, Reservation Service, Identity Verification Service, Room Service, Stay Service, and database. Include rejections for unconfirmed reservations, unsuitable or unavailable rooms, and inactive stays at check-out.

Prompt 4 — Activity Diagram and room assignment rules

Create a PlantUML Activity Diagram with Receptionist and System partitions for the same workflow. At check-in, only assign a room of the reserved type that is ready, not Out-of-Order, and has no overlapping active stay. Record the staff action in an audit log. At check-out, record departure, complete the stay, and update room readiness.

Prompt 5 — Integration with billing and housekeeping

Review the check-out flow so it hands off to Billing to close the folio and add any early/late check-out charge, and to Housekeeping to update the room status. Keep the early/late check-out policy as a configurable rule because the exact times and fees are still being agreed.

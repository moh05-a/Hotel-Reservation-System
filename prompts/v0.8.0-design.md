1. Design Task

Design billing and simulated payments for the Hotel Reservation System.

The design must create a folio for each stay, calculate accommodation charges, allow staff to add itemized extra charges, apply a configurable tax in JOD, and generate an itemized invoice. It must also record every simulated payment, reversal and refund with a status, and support the mandatory booking payment from Increment 5, without real bank connections or card storage.

3. Main Design Decisions
A folio is created (or retrieved) when a checked-in stay is opened and starts with the accommodation charge: number of nights × the nightly rate snapshot taken at booking time.
Successful booking payments from Increment 5 are attached to the folio as credits, so the guest is never charged twice.
Only authorized staff can add extra charges, and only to an Open folio. Invalid quantities or prices and duplicate requestId values are rejected. Every change writes an audit record.
Currency is JOD with 3 decimal places. Tax uses a configurable rate and is calculated once on the subtotal: tax = round_half_up(subtotal × rate, 3), total = subtotal + tax.
An invoice stores a snapshot of its line items and a unique invoice number, so later changes do not alter an issued invoice.
Payment transactions move through Pending, Successful, Failed, Reversed, Refunded and NeedsReconciliation. A failed payment is kept on record, and the booking hold stays Pending until its original deadline.
If a payment succeeds but the booking cannot be confirmed (expired hold or failed commit), the payment is reversed. If the reversal result is unknown, it is marked NeedsReconciliation.
Refunds follow the booking policy: a full refund at least 24 hours before the 14:00 check-in, otherwise no refund. The refund can never exceed the amount paid minus earlier refunds.
At check-out (Increment 7), the folio is closed after any early/late check-out charge is added, and the balance due is shown.
All payments are simulated. No card details are stored and no real money moves.

6. Representative Prompts (Reconstructed)

The prompts below are cleaned reconstructions of the main design requests. They summarize the design workflow and are not verbatim conversation transcripts.

Prompt 1 — Billing scope

Design Increment 8 (Billing and Payments) using FR-BL-01–05. Explain how a folio is created for a stay, how accommodation charges are calculated from the nightly rate and number of nights, how staff add extra charges, and how an itemized invoice is produced. All payments are simulated, with no real bank or card storage.

Prompt 2 — Coordination with mandatory booking payment

Increment 5 requires a successful simulated payment before a booking is confirmed. Design how Billing records that booking payment, handles failed attempts and retries without double charging, and reverses a successful payment if the hold expired or the confirmation commit failed. Attach confirmed booking payments to the folio as credits.

Prompt 3 — Sequence Diagram

Create a PlantUML Sequence Diagram for billing. Show the Guest, Receptionist, Booking Service, Staff UI, Billing API, Folio Service, Pricing Service, Invoice Service, Simulated Payment Service, and database. Include booking payment, reversal, folio creation at check-in, extra charges, tax and invoice, additional payment, refund, and folio closing at check-out.

Prompt 4 — Tax, rounding and refunds

Define the tax and rounding rules in JOD with 3 decimal places. Use a configurable tax rate applied once to the subtotal with round-half-up. Define refund eligibility using the 24-hour cancellation policy before the 14:00 check-in, and make sure a refund never exceeds what was paid minus earlier refunds.

Prompt 5 — Payment State Diagram

Create a PlantUML State Diagram for a simulated payment transaction. Include Pending, Successful, Failed, Reversed, Refunded, and NeedsReconciliation. Keep it separate from the reservation states and show when a reversal or refund result is unknown.

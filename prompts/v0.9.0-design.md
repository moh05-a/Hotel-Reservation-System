# Increment 9 — AI-Assisted Design Record

**Version:** v0.9.0-design  
**Increment:** Housekeeping and Room Readiness  
**Requirements:** FR-OP-01–FR-OP-03  
**Owner:** Zein A. Sunoqrot

## 1. Design Task

Design the housekeeping workflow and physical room operational state model for the Hotel Reservation System. The design must track room readiness across operational states (`Dirty`, `Clean`, `Inspected`, `Occupied`, `Out-of-Order`), enforce valid state transitions, restrict check-in assignment strictly to verified `Inspected` rooms, and maintain an immutable audit trail of all status changes.

## 2. AI Assistance

Gemini was used to assist with:

- Separating physical room operational readiness states from reservation lifecycle states (`FR-OP-01`–`FR-OP-03`).
- Creating the Room Operational State Diagram using PlantUML (`uml/state_room.puml`).
- Defining transition rules, validation errors, and check-in eligibility constraints.
- Designing the `RoomStatusHistory` audit schema to support both staff actions and automated system transitions.
- Documenting cross-increment coordination points with Availability (Increment 3) and Check-in Room Assignment (Increment 7).

## 3. Main Design Decisions

- Physical room operational states (`Dirty`, `Clean`, `Inspected`, `Occupied`, `Out-of-Order`) are modeled independently from reservation states (`Pending`, `Confirmed`, `CheckedIn`, etc.).
- Only rooms in `Inspected` state (with `is_active = true` and no overlapping active stay) are eligible for check-in assignment (`FR-OP-03`).
- Standard housekeeping cleaning transitions a room from `Dirty` to `Clean`, while supervisor verification is required to move from `Clean` to `Inspected`. Failed inspections or re-soiling return the room from `Clean` to `Dirty`.
- Guest check-out triggers an automatic system transition from `Occupied` to `Dirty`.
- Placing a room into `Out-of-Order` requires a mandatory reason and excludes it from immediate check-in; resolving maintenance transitions the room to `Dirty` so it undergoes cleaning and inspection before reuse.
- Every state change is recorded in `RoomStatusHistory` with `actor_type` (`STAFF` or `SYSTEM`), allowing `changed_by_staff_id` to be `NULL` for automated system transitions such as `AUTO_CHECKOUT`.
- Handling of future `Out-of-Order` periods on advance inventory blocks and pre-existing confirmed reservations is documented for coordination with Increments 3 and 7.

## 6. Representative Prompts (Reconstructed)

The prompts below are cleaned reconstructions of the main design requests. They summarize the design workflow and are **not verbatim conversation transcripts**.

**Prompt 1 — Housekeeping workflow and operational states**

> Design Increment 9 (Housekeeping and Room Readiness) using FR-OP-01–03. Separate physical room operational states from reservation states. Define the lifecycle across Dirty, Clean, Inspected, Occupied, and Out-of-Order, and explain the allowed transitions and role permissions.

**Prompt 2 — Room Operational State Diagram**

> Create a PlantUML State Diagram for physical room operational readiness. Include Dirty, Clean, Inspected, Occupied, and Out-of-Order states, room creation and deactivation transitions, supervisor inspection pass/fail paths, check-in from Inspected only, automatic check-out from Occupied to Dirty, and maintenance resolution returning to Dirty.

**Prompt 3 — Check-in assignment and Out-of-Order rules**

> Define the eligibility rules for assigning a room at check-in under FR-OP-03. Enforce that only active Inspected rooms with no overlapping stay can be assigned, require a mandatory reason when marking a room Out-of-Order, and specify invalid transitions that must be rejected with 400 Bad Request.

**Prompt 4 — Audit history for staff and system actions**

> Design the RoomStatusHistory audit table to record every room state transition. Include room ID, previous and new status, timestamp, reason, and actor tracking that supports both human staff updates and automated system events like guest check-out.

**Prompt 5 — Cross-increment coordination and documentation**

> Document the Increment 9 housekeeping rules in a concise Markdown specification. Clarify the immediate exclusion of Out-of-Order rooms at check-in while noting that future inventory block adjustments and pre-existing confirmed reservations require coordination with Increments 3 and 7.
